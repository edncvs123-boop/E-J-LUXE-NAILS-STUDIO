About Us

Eden & Jhoan

E&J Luxe Nails Studio® | Founder

E&J Luxe Nails Studio was proudly founded by Eden Cuevas and Jhoan De Villa, and officially established in August 2022 with a shared passion for beauty, creativity, and excellent customer care. What started as a simple vision has grown into a trusted nail salon dedicated to providing high-quality services in a clean, relaxing, and welcoming environment. We believe that nail care is more than just a beauty service—it is a form of self-expression and self-care that helps boost confidence and enhance individuality. From the moment you enter our studio, our goal is to make you feel comfortable, valued, and truly pampered.

At E&J Luxe Nails Studio, we are committed to delivering exceptional nail care services with precision, creativity, and attention to detail. We offer a wide range of services, including classic manicures and pedicures, gel polish, nail extensions, and customized nail art designed to match each client’s unique style and preferences. Using high-quality products and modern techniques, we ensure long-lasting and beautiful results while maintaining strict hygiene and sanitation standards for your safety and peace of mind. Our team continuously improves their skills and stays updated with the latest trends in the beauty industry to provide you with stylish, elegant, and up-to-date nail designs.

Our goal is to be recognized as a trusted and loved nail salon, known for our creativity, quality, and customer satisfaction. We strive to create a relaxing environment where every visit is enjoyable, leaving our clients feeling confident, refreshed, and truly pampered.

Vision Statement:

To be recognized as a premier, trusted, and deeply loved nail salon known for our unwavering commitment to exceptional quality, creativity, and outstanding customer care. E&J Luxe Nails Studio aspires to be more than just a beauty destination—we aim to become a sanctuary where clients can relax, feel valued, and enjoy services that enhance not only their appearance but also their confidence and overall well-being. We envision a consistently welcoming and luxurious environment where every visit is a memorable and satisfying experience, building lasting relationships founded on trust, comfort, and excellence.

Mission Statement:

Our mission at E&J Luxe Nails Studio is to provide high-quality nail care services that combine creativity, precision, and professionalism. We are dedicated to delivering exceptional results through the use of premium products, modern techniques, and strict hygiene and sanitation practices to ensure the safety and satisfaction of every client. We strive to create a relaxing and friendly atmosphere where clients can unwind and feel pampered while receiving personalized services tailored to their unique style and preferences. We are committed to continuous learning and improvement, staying updated with the latest trends and innovations in the beauty industry to provide modern and long-lasting nail solutions. Above all, we aim to build strong and lasting relationships with our clients by offering consistent, reliable, and excellent service every time they visit.

Core Values:

Excellence • Creativity • Customer Satisfaction • Cleanliness and Safety • Professionalism • Passion and Dedication
